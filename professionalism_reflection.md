# Professionalism at Focus Bear

## Reflection on Professionalism

During my experiences in both school projects and casual work settings, I’ve noticed that professionalism isn’t about formality—it’s about respect, clarity, and accountability. Great teamwork happens when everyone listens, communicates openly, and treats each other kindly, even in relaxed environments. For example, when a team I was part of needed to coordinate on a complex task, the members who stayed calm, clearly explained their ideas, and encouraged questions made collaboration smooth and productive.

To maintain clear, respectful, and inclusive communication, I plan to:
- Think before sending messages or responding in meetings to ensure tone is respectful
- Focus on facts and actions rather than personal attributes when giving or receiving feedback
- Assume good intent and ask for clarification if I don’t understand something

Creating a positive and professional environment can include small actions like celebrating team wins, acknowledging others’ contributions, and sharing ideas constructively. Preparing for meetings will involve reviewing notes, setting goals for what I need to discuss, and actively listening to colleagues and supervisors. Being proactive during my internship could mean:
- Asking clarifying questions before starting tasks
- Offering help to teammates when appropriate
- Following up politely if I need updates or actions from colleagues

If follow-ups do not get a response, I would escalate first to my supervisor, and only contact higher management in cases of serious breaches of professional conduct or urgent issues.

Some behaviours I will avoid include:
- Gossiping, bullying, or exclusion
- Sending rushed or unclear messages
- Reacting defensively to feedback

By focusing on respect, clarity, and accountability, I can contribute to a professional, fun, and inclusive work culture at Focus Bear.
