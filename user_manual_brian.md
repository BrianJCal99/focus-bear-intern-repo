## How I Work Best

I do my best work when:
- I clearly understand what the task is and what the end goal looks like
- I can focus without too many interruptions
- I have some time to think through problems on my own before asking for help
- There’s a balance between guidance and independence

I struggle when:
- Instructions are too vague or unclear
- I get stuck on something for too long without direction
- There are constant interruptions during focused work time

## Communication Style

I prefer messages to be:
- Clear and direct, but still friendly
- Detailed enough so I don’t have to guess what’s needed

The best way to reach me is:
- Discord or messaging or text messages for general communication
- Scheduled meetings for more complex discussions

If you need something urgent, do this:
- Send me a direct message and mention that it’s urgent

## Collaboration & Teamwork

I work best in a team when:
- Everyone is clear on their responsibilities
- People communicate openly and help each other when needed
- There’s a good balance between working independently and collaborating

My biggest pet peeve in teamwork is:
- Lack of communication or unclear expectations

If I seem quiet in a meeting, it usually means:
- I’m processing the information or thinking things through before speaking

## Focus & Productivity

I stay focused by:
- Working in a quiet environment
- Breaking tasks into smaller steps
- Listening to music sometimes while working

Things that distract me:
- Noise or frequent interruptions
- Switching between tasks too often

The best way to support my productivity is:
- Giving clear instructions and expectations
- Allowing time for focused work without interruptions
