# Data Privacy & Confidentiality Reflection

## Key Takeaways
- Confidential data at Focus Bear includes user personal information, health and behavioral data, internal company documents, and any information not meant for public disclosure.
- Handling confidential data responsibly is essential to maintain trust and comply with legal obligations.

## Best Practices for Handling Confidential Data
- Always store sensitive data in secure systems with appropriate access controls.
- Share information only with authorized colleagues and through approved channels.
- Dispose of confidential information safely, using secure deletion methods or shredding physical documents.
- Be vigilant for phishing attempts, suspicious requests, or accidental exposures.

## Responding to a Suspected Data Breach
- Report any suspected data breach immediately to the appropriate Focus Bear contact or security team.
- Avoid attempting to fix the issue on your own; follow company protocols.
- Document what happened to help with investigation and mitigation.

## Personal Reflection & Action Plan
- I will make a habit of double-checking the recipient before sharing any sensitive data via email or messaging tools.
- I will use encrypted storage for all files containing confidential information.
- I will review and stay updated on Focus Bear’s privacy policies regularly.
- I will avoid keeping sensitive information on personal devices or unsecured platforms.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Sending sensitive data to the wrong person or via insecure channels.
- Storing confidential information on personal or unencrypted devices.
- Failing to report accidental disclosures or breaches immediately.

**Key Learning / Security Measure to Implement:**  
If I have to work with private and confidential data, I will adopt the habit of encrypting sensitive files and using secure channels for sharing, ensuring that all confidential data remains protected and accessible only to authorized personnel.
