# OHS Reflection: Laptop Ergonomics

## Equipment Changes
To improve my workspace setup at home, I introduced a **monitor stand** to elevate my monitor screen closer to eye level and started using an **external keyboard and mouse**. This allows my wrists to remain in a neutral position and reduces hunching over the laptop. I also adjusted my **chair height** so that my feet rest flat on the floor and my knees are at approximately 90 degrees.

## Behavioural Changes
I focused on improving posture and reducing strain by:
- Sitting upright with shoulders relaxed.
- Keeping my screen at eye level to avoid neck strain.
- Taking **short movement breaks every 45–60 minutes**.
- Stretching my wrists, neck, and shoulders during breaks.

## Reminders and Habits
I set up **Focus Bear reminders** for movement and posture checks throughout the day. This helped me become more aware of slouching and encouraged regular stretches. Over the course of the day, I noticed **less neck tension and fewer wrist aches**, which shows the adjustments are effective.

## Key Takeaways
- Elevating the external monitor screen and using external input devices significantly improves posture.
- Regular movement breaks are essential for reducing strain.
- Consistent reminders help maintain ergonomic habits and prevent fatigue.
