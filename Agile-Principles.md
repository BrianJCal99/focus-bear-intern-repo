# Working in an Agile Team

## Research & Learn

### What is Agile, and how does it differ from traditional project management?

Agile is a flexible and iterative way of working where teams build, test, and improve products in small steps rather than trying to deliver everything at once. It focuses on adapting to change, getting feedback early, and continuously improving.

Traditional project management (like Waterfall) is more rigid. It follows a fixed sequence: planning → designing → building → testing → delivery. Changes are harder to make once the project has started.

---

### What are the core values and principles of Agile?

The Agile Manifesto highlights four core values:

* Individuals and interactions over processes and tools
* Working software over comprehensive documentation
* Customer collaboration over contract negotiation
* Responding to change over following a plan

There are also 12 principles, but the key ideas include:

* Delivering value frequently
* Welcoming changing requirements
* Close collaboration between teams and stakeholders
* Continuous improvement

---

### What are the differences between Scrum and Kanban, and when is each used?

Scrum:

* Work is divided into fixed time periods called sprints (usually 1–2 weeks)
* Teams have defined roles (Scrum Master, Product Owner, Developers)
* Planning and review meetings happen regularly
* Best for projects with structured goals and predictable cycles

Kanban:

* Work flows continuously without fixed timeframes
* Tasks are visualised on a board (e.g., To Do → In Progress → Done)
* No strict roles or required meetings
* Focuses on limiting work in progress and improving flow
* Best for ongoing work and teams that need flexibility

---

### Why does Focus Bear lean towards Kanban instead of Scrum?

Focus Bear likely prefers Kanban because the work is continuous and can change quickly. Instead of waiting for the next sprint, tasks can be updated, added, or completed at any time. This makes it easier to respond to user feedback, fix issues quickly, and stay flexible in a fast-moving environment.

## Reflection

### What do you think are the biggest benefits and challenges of Agile?

One of the biggest benefits of Agile is flexibility. It allows teams to adapt quickly when priorities change or when new information comes in. It also encourages regular feedback, which helps improve the final product and reduces the risk of building something that doesn’t meet user needs.

A challenge with Agile is that it can sometimes feel less structured, especially compared to traditional methods. Without clear planning or deadlines, it can be harder to stay organised or know exactly what to focus on. It also requires strong communication within the team, which can be difficult if not everyone is aligned.

---

### How can Agile principles help improve your role and responsibilities?

Agile principles can help me stay more adaptable in my work. Instead of trying to complete everything perfectly in one go, I can focus on making progress in smaller steps and improving over time.

It also encourages me to communicate more with teammates, ask for feedback earlier, and adjust my work based on that feedback. This is especially useful when working on tasks I’m less confident in, like backend coding, where early feedback can prevent bigger issues later.
