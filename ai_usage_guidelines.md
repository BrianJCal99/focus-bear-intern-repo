# Use of AI Tools – Reflection

## Understanding AI in My Role

AI tools are useful for things like drafting responses, brainstorming ideas, summarising content, and helping with coding or troubleshooting. I see them as a way to speed up tasks and get a starting point when I’m stuck.

## Benefits and Risks

The biggest benefit of AI is saving time and improving efficiency. It can help generate ideas quickly and make work easier. However, it’s not always accurate, and sometimes the information can be misleading or wrong. There’s also a risk of relying on it too much or accidentally sharing sensitive information.

## Sensitive Information

I understand that I should never enter confidential company data, user information, passwords, API keys, or any private internal documents into AI tools. Keeping this information secure is important to avoid any data breaches.

## Validating AI Output

I would always double-check AI-generated content before using it. This means reviewing it carefully, fact-checking important details, and making sure it actually makes sense. I wouldn’t just copy and paste the output without understanding it.

## When to Use AI vs My Own Skills

I would use AI to help with things like brainstorming, drafting, or learning something new. For tasks that require deeper understanding or accuracy, I would rely more on my own knowledge and thinking.

## Avoiding Over-Reliance

To avoid depending too much on AI, I would try to complete tasks on my own first before using it. I would also make sure to edit and improve anything AI generates instead of using it as-is.

## Data Privacy

To protect data, I will be careful about what I share with AI tools and follow any company guidelines. I will only use AI in ways that don’t put sensitive information at risk.

## Practical Task

During a personal project, I needed help with backend coding, which is an area I don’t have much experience in. I used AI to guide me through writing some of the code and understanding how it should work. It was helpful for getting started, but I didn’t fully rely on it. After generating the code, I reviewed it with a friend who has more experience to make sure it was secure and followed good practices. This helped me catch things I wouldn’t have noticed on my own and made me more confident in the final result.

## Best Practice

One best practice I will follow is to always review and verify AI-generated content before using it.
