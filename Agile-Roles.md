# Roles in an Agile Team

## Research & Learn

An Agile team consists of several key roles that work together to deliver a product efficiently. Developers are responsible for building and maintaining the product. In my case, I focus on frontend and mobile app development using React and React Native, where I implement user interfaces and ensure the application is responsive and user-friendly. QA (Quality Assurance) is responsible for testing features, identifying bugs, and ensuring the product meets quality standards. The Product Manager defines the product vision, prioritises tasks, and translates user needs into clear requirements. The Agile Project Manager or Scrum Master facilitates Agile processes, supports the team, and removes any blockers that may affect progress. Supporting roles such as UI/UX designers, stakeholders, and customer support also play an important part by contributing design, feedback, and user insights.

In Scrum, work is organised into fixed-length sprints with clearly defined goals and regular ceremonies such as planning, stand-ups, and retrospectives. Collaboration is structured and happens within each sprint cycle. In contrast, Kanban focuses on continuous delivery, where tasks move through a workflow without fixed deadlines. Collaboration is more flexible, and team members pick up work based on priority and capacity.

## Reflection

My role is a frontend and mobile app developer working with React and React Native. I contribute to the Agile team by building user-facing features and ensuring that the application is both functional and visually consistent. My work involves translating UI/UX designs into working components and improving the overall user experience of the product.

I frequently collaborate with UI/UX designers to understand design requirements and ensure accurate implementation. I also work closely with QA to identify and fix bugs, making sure that features meet quality expectations. Additionally, I interact with the Product Manager when clarification is needed on requirements or expected outcomes.

## Task

As a frontend and mobile developer, my role fits into the Agile team by focusing on delivering the parts of the product that users interact with directly. I take design concepts and turn them into functional features, ensuring they perform well across devices and meet user expectations. I also contribute to the Agile process by communicating progress, raising issues early, and adapting to feedback from the team.

One role I frequently collaborate with is UI/UX designers. To improve teamwork, I can communicate earlier when there are technical limitations or uncertainties in the design. Asking questions before starting development can help avoid misunderstandings, and providing feedback on feasibility can lead to better design decisions. I can also improve collaboration with QA by testing my work more thoroughly before submission, clearly communicating expected behaviour, and addressing bugs promptly.
